About me

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from NITK Surathkal, where I completed my undergraduate degree with a CGPA of 9.15/10. During my final year, I undertook an academic exchange in the Aerospace Engineering department at IIT Madras and carried out my final-year research project there. This experience helped shape my interest in fluid mechanics and aerodynamics, particularly in problems where computational analysis can be supported by carefully designed experiments.

At the Geophysical Flows Laboratory (GFL), IIT Madras, I began as a research intern and subsequently worked as a Project Associate. My work centred on rotor-induced flow around unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), motivated by the challenges of making reliable atmospheric wind measurements from rotorcraft. I worked with a Fortran-based free-vortex wake model to study the induced velocity field of single-rotor and multi-rotor arrangements. I also developed post-processing workflows to examine and visualise the computed flow fields, compare configurations and identify regions with relatively low rotor-induced disturbance that may be suitable for wind-sensor placement.

The computational work was complemented by experimental velocity measurements and validation of the model for a single-rotor configuration. The wider study considered different UAV layouts and flight conditions, including hover and descent scenarios. I then helped bring the modelling, validation and sensor-placement findings together as a research article, preparing the manuscript in LaTeX and taking it through the journal submission and revision process. The resulting article has been accepted for final publication in Atmospheric Measurement Techniques (AMT).

My earlier experience at NITK broadened my exposure to heat transfer, thermal management, structural mechanics and vehicle design. Through undergraduate research, IEEE NITK activities and the NITKRacing team, I gained experience in CFD, finite-element analysis, CAD, MATLAB-based modelling and technical mentoring. I have also conducted introductory knowledge-exchange sessions on CAD, FEA, MATLAB and Simulink.

I am particularly interested in research and engineering problems involving fluid mechanics, aerodynamics, turbulence, computational modelling and experimental validation. I enjoy combining physical understanding with numerical tools, and I am keen to continue developing rigorous approaches to analysing complex flow and engineering systems.

Publications

Aerodynamic evaluation of wind speed sensor placement on UAVs for meteorological applications

Ashish Pastay et al. · 2026

Accepted for final publication

Atmospheric Measurement Techniques (AMT), Copernicus Publications, on behalf of the European Geosciences Union (EGU).

This study uses a free-vortex wake model to investigate rotor-induced velocity fields around single-rotor, quad-rotor and hexa-rotor UAV configurations under different flight conditions. The work examines the influence of rotor wakes on wind measurements and identifies aerodynamic quiet zones that can inform the placement of wind-speed sensors for meteorological applications. The numerical model is also assessed against single-rotor experimental measurements.
