Academic and research background

I completed my undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering at NITK Surathkal with a CGPA of 9.15/10. During my final year, I undertook an academic exchange in the Aerospace Engineering department at IIT Madras, where I carried out my final-year research project. This experience developed my interest in fluid mechanics and aerodynamics, particularly in combining computational analysis with experimental investigation.

At the Geophysical Flows Laboratory, IIT Madras, I began as a research intern and subsequently worked as a Project Associate. My work focused on the numerical investigation of rotor-induced flow fields around single- and multi-rotor UAVs. I worked with a Fortran-based free-vortex wake model and developed post-processing workflows to study flow fields generated by single- and multi-rotor configurations.

I also contributed to experimental velocity measurements and validation of the numerical model for a single-rotor configuration. The wider study considered different rotor arrangements and flight conditions to identify low-disturbance regions relevant to wind-sensor placement.

The findings were brought together into a research article prepared using LaTeX and submitted to Atmospheric Measurement Techniques (AMT), where the manuscript has been accepted for final publication.

My undergraduate activities at NITK broadened my experience in heat transfer, thermal management, structural analysis and vehicle design. Through IEEE NITK and NITKRacing, I also gained experience in technical mentoring, engineering design and collaborative project work.

Rotor-induced flow fields around UAVs

Geophysical Flows Laboratory · IIT Madras

Investigated the flow disturbances generated by rotating propellers and their implications for atmospheric wind measurements using UAV platforms.

  • Studied single-rotor, quad-rotor and hexa-rotor configurations.
  • Examined rotor-induced velocity distributions under hover and descent flight conditions.
  • Identified aerodynamic quiet zones for potential wind-sensor placement.
  • Compared numerical predictions with single-rotor experimental measurements.

This research formed the basis of my journal article in Atmospheric Measurement Techniques.

Numerical modelling and post-processing

Worked with a Fortran-based free-vortex wake model for evaluating rotor-induced flow fields. The computational work involved analysing the spatial distribution of induced velocity around rotor systems and post-processing the numerical results for physical interpretation.

Swiss-Roll Liquid-Cooling Microchannel for EV Battery Thermal Management

IEEE NITK · Mentored project

Investigated Swiss-roll liquid-cooling microchannel designs for electric-vehicle battery modules under high-power operating conditions, such as vehicle acceleration on inclined surfaces.

Different microchannel flow configurations were considered under comparable coolant properties, inlet temperature and flow-rate conditions to assess heat dissipation and temperature uniformity, with the broader objective of reducing overheating and mitigating thermal-runaway risks.

Battery Thermal Management Microchannels Heat Transfer

Structural Analysis and Material Selection of Gears

IEEE NITK · Mentored project

Performed static structural analysis of meshing spur, helical and bevel gears. Compared the mechanical behaviour of cast iron, bronze, brass and aluminium alloys to evaluate their suitability for gear applications.
